Privacy Policy for Reps.io

Last Updated: December 6, 2025

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Reps.io ("we," "our," or "the App") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains that we do not collect, use, or share any of your personal data.

2. No Data Collection

Reps.io is designed to operate entirely locally on your device.

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information (such as your name, email address, or location) on external servers.
  • We do not use third-party analytics or tracking tools that share your data with advertisers or other third parties.

3. Local Data Storage

All data you enter into the App—including your workout plans, exercise history, body measurements, and login credentials—is stored locally on your device using Apple's Core Data technology.

  • This data remains on your device and is not accessible to the developer.
  • If you delete the App, this local data will be permanently removed from your device (unless you have backed up your device via iCloud or iTunes).

4. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party services that collect data. It relies solely on the standard iOS environment provided by Apple.

5. Children’s Privacy

Since we do not collect any data, we do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
